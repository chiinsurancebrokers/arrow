War, Unrest & Kidnap Exclusion — Certificate CGT P804302600

Endorsements, Certificate CGT P804302600

Cover under this Certificate excludes all travel to areas of War, Unrest or Disruption unless declared and agreed by Underwriters prior to travelling to such areas.

Area of War, Unrest or Disruption is defined as:

  • Business travel: a country/region the FCDO advises against "ALL" travel, plus Belarus, Russia, Ukraine, Gaza, Israel & Lebanon by name.
  • Holiday travel (only relevant if the Holiday Inclusion Clause applies to a person — currently Not Included on this certificate): a country/region the FCDO advises against "ALL" or "All but Essential" travel, plus the same six named countries.

Kidnap exclusion. Cover excludes any Kidnap occurring in Afghanistan, Belarus, Colombia, Gaza, Israel, Iraq, Lebanon, Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Philippines, Russia, Somalia, Ukraine, Venezuela, Yemen — or any other country/region where the UK FCDO has advised against "all travel" or "all travel due to the threat of kidnap" prior to the trip starting.

Any One Accident/Incident aggregate cap. Underwriters' total liability for all Insured Persons suffering Bodily Injury in the same Accident, or series of Accidents from one cause, is capped at €10,000,000. If one incident exceeds this, each Insured Person's benefit is reduced proportionately.

Always check current FCDO travel advice for the specific destination before booking or travelling — this is the trigger for the exclusion, and it can change at short notice.

ℹ️ About the Travel Tracker

Purpose

The Arrow Travel Tracker is an informational system designed to help Arrow Shipping Company monitor employee travel days against the annual 250-day policy limit.

How It Works

  • Tracks individual employee trips and dates
  • Calculates cumulative days across all employees
  • Shows remaining days available under the policy
  • Marks completed and canceled trips
  • Provides real-time visibility into travel utilization

🤖 Meet HAL

HAL is a chat assistant, available from the "Ask HAL" button, that explains Arrow's travel certificate (CGT P804302600) in plain language — things like cover limits, exclusions, and what to do in an emergency.

  • HAL only answers from the actual wording of this certificate — it doesn't guess, round, or use general insurance knowledge as if it were Arrow's terms.
  • If something isn't in the certificate (like the 250-day tracking pool, which is an Arrow/CHI convention, not a policy figure), HAL says so directly rather than making something up.
  • HAL doesn't handle claims. For an active claim or emergency, use the contacts under Emergency Contacts instead — Healix (medical), Constellis (kidnap/security), or Crawford TPA (claims).

System Features

Color-coded Progress Bars:

  • 🟢 Green: Under 40% utilized
  • 🔵 Blue: 40-60% utilized
  • 🟡 Yellow: 60-80% utilized
  • 🔴 Red: Over 80% utilized

Trip Status Indicators:

  • Blue background: Upcoming/current trips
  • Green background + ✓: Completed trips
  • Red background + ✗: Canceled trips (not counted)
